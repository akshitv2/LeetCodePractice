|------------------------------------------------|-----------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------|
| Longest Substring Without Repeating Characters | Efficient | (Sliding Window with Map/Array) Maintain a window of unique characters; when a duplicate is found, jump the `start` pointer to the right of the duplicate's last seen position.  <br/> Use an array/hashmap to store `index + 1` of characters, ensuring `start` never moves backward via `Math.max()`. | $O(N)$ time, $O(1)$ space |
